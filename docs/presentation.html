01. Industry Challenge & Problem Statement
Healthcare IT Gaps
Inter-Facility Referral Disconnect
Rural PHCs and Sub-Centers lack direct clinical communication channels with District Hospitals. Critical referrals face transit delays, missing baseline vitals, and unreserved ICU beds on arrival.
High-Concurrency Double Bookings
Standard clinic portals suffer from race conditions: multiple simultaneous patient requests book the same specialist slot at identical milliseconds, leading to clinic overbooking and physician burnout.
Chaotic Waiting Rooms & Queue Anxiety
Paper tokens and verbal caller shouting cause immense waiting room congestion, missed patient turns, and zero real-time visibility into consultation queues.
Fragmented EHR & Audit Non-Compliance
Patient health data is isolated in siloed registers with no cryptographically timestamped HIPAA/GDPR audit trail to monitor unauthorized medical record access.

05. Concurrency-Safe Appointment Booking
Double-Booking Prevention

Double-booking prevention is enforced at the database row level using JPA Pessimistic Locking (PESSIMISTIC_WRITE) executing SELECT ... FOR UPDATE.

@Lock(LockModeType.PESSIMISTIC_WRITE) @Query("SELECT s FROM DoctorSlot s " + "WHERE s.id = :slotId " + "AND s.isBooked = false") Optional<DoctorSlot> findSlotForBookingWithLock( @Param("slotId") UUID slotId );
Thread 1 (Patient A) — 200 OK
Acquires exclusive write lock on slot row. Transitions isBooked = true. Appointment created. Transaction commits safely.
Thread 2 (Patient B) — 409 CONFLICT
Query blocks until Thread 1 commits. Re-evaluates isBooked = false, detects slot taken, and immediately throws SlotAlreadyBookedException.

07. 7-Stage Inter-Facility Referral Bridge
Referral Setu Protocol

Standardizes clinical handovers between primary health centers and tertiary district hospitals, eliminating transit communication drops:

1. Initiated
Triage created at PHC
2. Triaged
Urgency scored (Red/Yellow)
3. Transport Booked
108 unit reserved
4. Dispatched
En-route with telemetry
5. Arrived at Facility
Reception check-in & bed allocated
6. Specialist Consult
Active physician evaluation
7. Completed / Admitted
Admitted to ICU/Ward or resolved
